CUSTOM SONG & PHOTOS GUIDE / APOLOGY SITE:

METHOD 1 (Easiest - Direct In-Browser Upload):
- Click on the "Upload Photos & Song" button in the website header or bottom widget.
- Select your MP3 file directly from your phone or PC.
- Select your photos for the Hero avatar and Polaroid memories.
- Click "Save & Update Site ❤️" - it will instantly play your song and display your pictures!

METHOD 2 (Static Files in /public folder):
- Put your song file named: `song.mp3` in this `/public` folder.
- Put your photos named:
  - `hero.jpg` (for the round profile picture)
  - `photo1.jpg` (Photo 1)
  - `photo2.jpg` (Photo 2)
  - `photo3.jpg` (Photo 3)
  - `photo4.jpg` (Photo 4)
inside this `/public` folder.
The website will automatically load them!
